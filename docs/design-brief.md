# Design Brief — Initial Direction Exploration

## Desired perception

Contemporary Argentine tango with cultural depth, sensuality without cliché, nocturnal atmosphere, movement, human connection and a strong sense of place.

## Avoid

- generic event-site layouts
- fake luxury aesthetics
- excessive red/black clichés
- stock silhouettes of couples
- vintage wallpaper treatment everywhere
- uniform cards for every piece of information
- giant AI-gradient hero art
- decorative motion with no narrative purpose

## Candidate visual families

### A — Editorial Night
Ink/black field, warm paper typography, cinematic photography, asymmetrical editorial grid, vertical type moments, slow image reveals.

### B — Urban Milonga
Concrete/charcoal base, brighter typographic system, sharper geometry, venue-map cues, poster-like blocks, rhythmic transitions.

### C — Contemporary Stage
Deep neutral field with theatrical spotlight logic, large negative space, kinetic typography, dramatic cropping, motion-driven reveals.

### D — Archivo Vivo
Paper/ink foundation with contemporary interventions, archival textures used sparingly, timeline motifs, editorial annotations and modern typography.

These are starting families, not final designs. The first design prompt should explore all of them enough to compare meaningfully.

## Typography direction

Start by testing a high-character display family against a clean reading face. Candidate pairings can include Cormorant Garamond / Manrope or Bodoni Moda / Inter, but do not lock the final pairing without visual comparison.

## Motion direction

Use choreography inspired by tango: approach, pause, weight shift, reveal, sweep, settle. Avoid constant scrolling effects.
